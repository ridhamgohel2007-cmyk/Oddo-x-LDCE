# 🎬 GlobeTrotter — Hackathon Submission Video Script (3-Minute Presentation)

**Project Name:** GlobeTrotter — Smart Multi-City Travel Platform  
**Target Duration:** 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)  
**Tone:** Energetic, professional, and product-focused  

---

## 🎥 Video Outline & Scene-by-Scene Script

### ⏱️ Scene 1: Hook & Problem Statement (0:00 – 0:25)
- **Visual:** Screen recording of browser tabs full of travel sites, spreadsheets, and messy notes.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Planning multi-city travel is chaotic. Travelers struggle to organize day-by-day stops, balance budgets across destinations, and find authentic, reusable itineraries created by real travelers."*
- **Visual Transition:** Cut to GlobeTrotter's high-contrast dark mode dashboard hero banner.

---

### ⏱️ Scene 2: Introducing GlobeTrotter (0:25 – 0:50)
- **Visual:** Dashboard tour showing active trip countdowns, multi-continent filters (Asia, Europe, Americas, etc.), and travel vibe pills.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Meet GlobeTrotter — an enterprise-grade, multi-city travel management platform built with React, TypeScript, Express, and Prisma SQLite. GlobeTrotter streamlines itinerary creation, expense tracking, and community sharing into one seamless experience."*

---

### ⏱️ Scene 3: Multi-City Itinerary Builder (0:50 – 1:20)
- **Visual:** Clicking into a trip (e.g., Grand European Voyage). Show adding city stops (Paris ➡️ Amsterdam ➡️ Rome), setting dates, and organizing activities per day.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"With our Multi-City Builder, travelers can chain destinations, schedule daily sightseeing, dining, and transport, and export full travel guides as PDFs with a single click."*

---

### ⏱️ Scene 4: Financial View & Multi-Currency Analytics (1:20 – 1:45)
- **Visual:** Navigate to Financial View. Show Recharts expense donut chart, category breakdowns (Stay, Transport, Activities, Meals), live currency switcher (₹ INR / $ USD / € EUR), and overbudget alerts.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Travelers get real-time financial control. Compare estimated budgets against logged receipts, switch between currencies dynamically, and receive instant overbudget warnings before overspending."*

---

### ⏱️ Scene 5: Community Hub & 1-Click Itinerary Cloning (1:45 – 2:10)
- **Visual:** Open Community Hub. Scroll through curated posts, click **Clone Itinerary**, and show it instantly populating into the user's account.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"GlobeTrotter brings travelers together. In the Community Hub, users publish custom travel guides. Found a trip you love? Clone the entire itinerary into your account in one click."*

---

### ⏱️ Scene 6: Calendar View & Admin Governance (2:10 – 2:45)
- **Visual:** Show Google Calendar-style month grid with trip pills, then switch to the Admin Panel showing user management and system metrics.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Track all your travel timelines visually in the Calendar View. Plus, role-based governance gives administrators full oversight to manage users, monitor platform health, and promote community safety."*

---

### ⏱️ Scene 7: Closing & Tech Stack (2:45 – 3:00)
- **Visual:** Final slide featuring GlobeTrotter logo, Tech Stack badges, and GitHub repository URL.
- **Voiceover:**  
  *"Powered by React 18, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Express, and Prisma SQLite — GlobeTrotter makes multi-city travel effortless. Thank you for reviewing our hackathon submission!"*

---

## 💡 Recording Tips & Checklist

1. **Screen Resolution:** Record at 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps.
2. **Audio:** Use a clean microphone with background noise cancellation.
3. **Cursor Highlights:** Enable subtle cursor highlight circle during clicks.
